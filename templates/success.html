Importing to Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar in your web browser.
  2. In the top right, click the Settings icon (Gear) > Settings.
  3. In the left menu, click Import & Export.
  4. Click Select file from your computer and choose the downloaded SyllySchedule.ics file.
  5. Choose which calendar to add the events to, then click Import.

Importing to Apple Calendar

  1. On Mac: Simply double-click the downloaded SyllySchedule.ics file. Apple Calendar will open and ask which calendar you want to add the events to.
  2. On iPhone/iPad: If you downloaded this on your computer, email the file to yourself or send it via AirDrop. Tap the file attachment on your mobile device to add all events.

Importing to Microsoft Outlook

  1. Desktop App: Double-click the SyllySchedule.ics file. Outlook will open and prompt you to "Import" or "Open as New". Choose Import to add them to your main calendar.
  2. Outlook on the Web: Go to your calendar, click Add calendar > Upload from file.
  3. Browse for the SyllySchedule.ics file, select your preferred calendar, and click Import.
